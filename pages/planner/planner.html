Chip Strategy & Information
Wildcard

Play to make unlimited free transfers. Your accumulated free transfers will not be reset.

Free Hit

Make unlimited transfers for a single Gameweek. Your squad reverts back for the next deadline.

Bench Boost

Points scored by your bench players are added to your Gameweek total.

Triple Captain

Your captain's points are multiplied by three instead of two.

  • Starting FTs: Set how many Free Transfers you carry into the first displayed Gameweek (e.g., 0 before GW1 deadline, 1 or more during the season).
  • Making Transfers: Click on a player's row in the grid for the Gameweek you want to make a transfer, then click the + button next to a player in the right sidebar.
  • Captains: Click [C] or [V] under a player's name to set your Captain and Vice-Captain.
  • Chips: Toggle chips in the Gameweek headers. The planner fully supports the 24/25 rules (chips do not reset accumulated FTs!).
