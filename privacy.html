Privacy Policy

Welcome to Open FPL Insights. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information unless explicitly provided by you. Any Fantasy Premier League data loaded into the app is either publicly available or stored locally in your browser.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information and data retrieved from the FPL API are used strictly to provide you with insights, planning tools, and dashboard functionalities within this application.

3. Local Storage

This application uses your browser's local storage to save your preferences, your team ID, and cached FPL data to improve performance and user experience. We do not transmit this local data to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools or APIs (such as the official FPL API) to fetch data. Their respective privacy policies govern the data retrieved from them.

5. Disclaimer

This application is an independent tool and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at websitedesignwarehouse@gmail.com.
